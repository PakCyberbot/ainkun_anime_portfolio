# Professional subscript logo variants

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each final prompt is the shared prompt followed by its individual direction.

## Shared prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE professional typographic monogram for a calm Japanese-inspired cybersecurity portfolio with a leafy morning theme. CRITICAL LAYOUT: a large lowercase Latin 'a' is the primary character; the Japanese kanji '刀' is a SMALL SUBSCRIPT at its LOWER RIGHT, like mathematical notation a with a subscript. The 刀 must be roughly 32 percent of the a's height, with its baseline below the a's baseline. Do not render a and 刀 as equal-size neighbors. Both characters must be recognizable, distinctly separated by a small optical gap. Exactly those two glyphs, no other text. Professional, carefully spaced, restrained and sophisticated; moderate small-radius corner softening, crisp typography and defined stroke endings, NOT bubbly, pill-shaped, cartoonish, overly rounded, or aggressively sharp. Deep forest green #49684a with optional restrained warm gold #b28b48. Flat opaque vector-like shapes, clean edges, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no gradients. No leaves, sun, stars, badges, decorative icons or long diagonal slashes. Transparent background with alpha, including the counters, no opaque rectangle or painted checkerboard. Center the full monogram with generous even margins on a square canvas, filling about 75 percent of the width.
