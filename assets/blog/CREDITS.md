Charming professional friendly expression, cozy slice-of-life Japanese anime aesthetic, refined clean ink outlines and soft cel shading, muted sage/cream/warm brown palette matching a relaxing leafy neighborhood. Front three-quarter view facing slightly right toward page content. Full body centered with a small transparent margin, clear silhouette legible at 140px tall. Genuine transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no ground plane, no text, no speech bubble, no logo, no watermark, no frame. Only one character, not a sprite sheet.
