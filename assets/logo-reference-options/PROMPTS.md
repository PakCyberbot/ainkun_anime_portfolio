## 1. Balanced Cut

File: `assets/logo-reference-options/01-balanced-cut.png`

Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE original professional logo concept based on the supplied reference image, for ainkun's calm Japanese anime neighborhood cybersecurity portfolio.
Reference role: letterform and identity inspiration ONLY. The reference is a screenshot of an italic lowercase a paired with Japanese 刀 and a diagonal gold accent.
Exact characters: lowercase Latin "a" followed by Japanese "刀". Retain the recognizable two-stroke structure of 刀, not 力 or a Latin letter. Make a dominant; place 刀 slightly smaller, around 65–70% of the a height, as a lower-right subscript, clearly readable.
Style: clean flat vector-like logo, disciplined custom typography, professional yet approachable. Balanced curves with modest corner treatment, controlled crisp terminals, no puffy bubble letters, no aggressive spikes. Harmonize with a wholesome morning street, cream panels and sage greenery.
Palette for these concept previews: forest/sage green #49684a and restrained muted gold #b28b48 only. Opaque solid strokes with crisp anti-aliased edges; no gradients, texture, glow, shadows, embossing, mockup or scenery.
Composition: one centered standalone logo, square canvas, mark occupies about 70% of canvas width with comfortable transparent margins. True transparent background. No cream rectangle, checkerboard, border, numbers, captions, words, cursor, hand pointer or screenshot artifacts.

Specific direction for this concept:
Preserve the reference's overall forward-leaning silhouette. A broad single-storey italic a with a clean open counter, small lower-right 刀, a fine straight gold diagonal seam ascending between them. Shorten the sharp endpoints so the accent is elegant rather than weapon-like. Refined compact spacing, gently eased corners with clearly defined flat terminals.
